Prospective Doctoral Research博士研究方向

Ph.D. Focus博士重点

My doctoral research centres on multimodal AI fusion for intelligent quality verification of radiological diagnosis. By integrating medical imaging, diagnostic report text and patient clinical data, it aims to accurately identify missed diagnosis, misdiagnosis and inconsistent diagnostic logic.

Built on my proven experience in clinical time-series modelling, medical image AI evaluation, full-stack medical informatics development and patented medical radiation device design, this work addresses the key limitation of current single-modal medical AI, evolving from passive lesion recognition to active cross-modal diagnostic logic supervision.

我的博士研究方向为多模态 AI 融合的放射诊断智能质控：通过整合医学影像、诊断报告文本与患者临床数据，旨在精准识别漏诊、误诊与不一致的诊断逻辑。

基于我在临床时间序列建模、医学影像 AI 评估、全栈医疗信息学开发及医疗辐射防护设备专利方面的实践积累，该研究直指当前单模态医疗 AI 的关键局限，从被动病灶识别走向主动的跨模态诊断逻辑监督。
